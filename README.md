# 🤖 Posture Study Assistant

> A smart desk robot that helps students study better — by keeping them comfortable, focused, and on task.

Built at **BearHack @ UC Riverside** by a team of 3.

## What It Does

The Posture Study Assistant is a desk-mounted robotic helper that monitors your study session in real time and gives you feedback before bad habits set in.

### Core Features
- **Posture Detection** — Camera + computer vision to detect slouching
- **Workspace Monitoring** — Light and noise sensors assess study environment
- **Task Tracking** — Prompts breaks and task transitions at the right times
- **Live Feedback** — Visual, auditory, or physical robotic cues
